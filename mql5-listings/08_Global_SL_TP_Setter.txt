2. SHORT MARKETPLACE INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Global Stop Loss and Target Manager is a MetaTrader 5 utility for
manual and discretionary traders who want to manage Stop Loss and
Take Profit levels across multiple positions on the same symbol.

Instead of modifying each position individually, the utility provides
one global Stop Loss line and one global Take Profit line on the chart.

Drag either line and the corresponding protection level of open
positions on the current symbol is updated to match it.

New positions opened on the same symbol automatically inherit the
current Stop Loss and Take Profit levels.

IMPORTANT: This is a position-management utility, not a trading
strategy. It does not generate trading signals, predict prices, or
guarantee profits.

5. HOW IT WORKS
============================================================

Global Stop Loss and Target Manager uses two horizontal lines on the
chart:

1. Global Stop Loss line
2. Global Take Profit line

5.1 Stop Loss

Drag the global Stop Loss line to the desired price.

The utility updates the Stop Loss of positions on the current symbol
to that level when the requested modification is valid.

5.2 Take Profit

Drag the global Take Profit line to the desired price.

The utility updates the Take Profit of positions on the current symbol
to that level when the requested modification is valid.

5.3 New Positions

When a new position is opened on the current symbol, the utility
applies the current global Stop Loss and Take Profit levels.

6. SAFE BY DESIGN
============================================================

The utility is designed specifically for position protection and
management.

It:

- Does not open trades.
- Does not close trades.

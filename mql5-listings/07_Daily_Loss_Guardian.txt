2. SHORT MARKETPLACE INTRODUCTION
=================================

Daily Loss Guardian is a MetaTrader 5 risk-management utility designed
to monitor account equity against configured daily-loss and maximum-
drawdown limits.

When a configured risk threshold is reached, the utility can close open
positions and prevent additional trading according to its risk-control
settings.

The utility also provides session-aware controls, economic-news
filtering, an on-chart risk dashboard, alerts and additional risk
calculation tools.

Daily Loss Guardian is designed for traders who want an independent
account-level risk layer that can operate alongside an existing Expert
Advisor or manual trading workflow.

IMPORTANT: Daily Loss Guardian is a risk-management utility, not a
trading strategy. It does not generate trading signals and does not
guarantee profits or prevent all trading losses.

The utility enforces the rules configured by the trader. Prop-firm
requirements can vary and may change, so traders must verify their
provider's current rules before configuring the utility.

5. HOW DAILY LOSS GUARDIAN WORKS
================================

Daily Loss Guardian operates as an account-level risk-management layer.

Basic workflow:

Read account state

↓

Determine configured daily-loss baseline

↓

Monitor current account equity

↓

Calculate current daily loss

↓

Compare equity against configured daily-loss limit

↓

Monitor maximum drawdown

↓

Monitor configured session/news conditions

↓

If configured risk threshold is reached:

Close positions according to configuration

↓

Block additional trading according to configuration

↓

Generate dashboard status and alerts

The utility is designed to run independently of the strategy generating
the trades.

============================================================
6. DAILY LOSS PROTECTION
========================

Daily Loss Guardian monitors account equity against the configured
daily-loss threshold.

The system is designed to account for floating profit and loss rather
than relying only on closed-trade balance.

When the configured daily-loss threshold is reached, the utility can:

* Close open positions.
* Prevent additional trading.
* Activate the configured kill-switch state.
* Display the risk status on the dashboard.
* Send configured alerts.

The exact behavior depends on the configured parameters and the
implementation of the current product version.

IMPORTANT: Traders should verify how their specific prop firm calculates
daily loss before configuring the utility.

Different firms can use different definitions involving balance,
equity, starting balance, daily reset time and other conditions.

============================================================
7. MAXIMUM DRAWDOWN PROTECTION
==============================

The second major protection layer monitors account-level drawdown.

When equity reaches the configured maximum-drawdown threshold, the
utility can:

* Close open positions.
* Stop additional trading.
* Activate the drawdown protection state.
* Display the current drawdown status.
* Trigger configured alerts.

This provides a second layer of protection in addition to the daily-loss
limit.

The utility does not determine whether a prop firm's drawdown definition
matches the configured value automatically.

Traders must verify their provider's current drawdown rules.

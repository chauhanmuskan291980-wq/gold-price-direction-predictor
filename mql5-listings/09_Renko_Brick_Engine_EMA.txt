2. SHORT MARKETPLACE INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Renko Brick Engine With EMA Overlay is a free MetaTrader 5 utility that
builds and tracks its own Renko brick series directly from price data.

The engine creates Renko bricks in memory, seeds the series with recent
historical data when attached, and calculates fast and slow EMAs directly
from Renko brick closes rather than time-based candle closes.

The utility displays the recent brick series and both EMA lines directly
on the chart, while also providing an information panel and recent-brick
logging for easier inspection of the engine's behaviour.

IMPORTANT: This is a visualization and analysis utility. It does not
open, close, or manage trades and does not provide a profitability
guarantee or trading performance claim.

5. HOW THE RENKO ENGINE WORKS
============================================================

The utility does not depend on an offline Renko chart.

When the utility is attached to a chart, it uses recent historical data to
seed the initial brick series and then continues tracking price through
live tick data.

The resulting Renko bricks are maintained in memory and used as the
basis for the chart display and EMA calculations.

5.1 Initial Seeding

The engine uses the configured history bars and timeframe to build an
initial Renko series when it is attached.

5.2 Live Brick Construction

After initialization, the engine continues processing live price
movement and adds new Renko bricks when the configured brick conditions
are met.

5.3 Confirmed Bricks

A brick is treated as confirmed when it closes. Confirmed bricks form
the in-memory Renko series used by the utility.

5.4 EMA Calculation

The fast and slow EMAs are calculated from Renko brick closing prices,
not from standard time-based candle closes.

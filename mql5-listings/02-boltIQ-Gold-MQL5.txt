2. SHORT MARKETPLACE INTRODUCTION
=================================

BoltIQ Gold EA is an automated MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor designed
primarily for XAUUSD (Gold) trading.

The EA combines a rule-based trend-following strategy with an optional
ONNX machine-learning filter. The core strategy uses EMA trend
detection, RSI momentum confirmation, ADX trend-strength filtering and
ATR-based volatility management.

The ML filter is optional. Traders can run the EA using the rule-based
strategy alone or enable the supplied ONNX model as an additional
trade-selection layer.

BoltIQ Gold EA also includes account-level risk controls such as daily
loss protection, maximum drawdown monitoring, session controls,
economic-news filtering, configurable risk sizing and position limits.

IMPORTANT: No specific historical performance figures are claimed
without a reproducible MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester report. Past
performance does not guarantee future results. Prop-firm rules vary
and must be checked by the trader before use.

5. HOW THE TRADING STRATEGY WORKS
=================================

BoltIQ Gold EA does not rely on a single indicator for its core entry
logic.

The rule-based strategy combines trend direction, momentum and trend
strength before considering an entry.

5.1 Trend Direction

The EA uses EMA(20) and EMA(50) to identify potential bullish or bearish
trend direction.

5.2 Momentum Confirmation

RSI(14) is used as a momentum filter.

Long setup: RSI(14) > 55

Short setup: RSI(14) < 45

5.3 Trend Strength Confirmation

ADX(14) must be above 25 before an entry is considered.

5.4 Volatility Management

ATR(14) is used to adapt stop-loss distance to current market
volatility.

5.5 Closed-Bar Signals

The product configuration specifies that the trading strategy uses
closed-bar information for its signals.

8. OPTIONAL ONNX MACHINE-LEARNING FILTER
========================================

The ML component is an optional additional filtering layer.

8.1 How It Works

The EA can:

1. Detect a setup using the core rule-based strategy.
2. Pass the setup features to the supplied ONNX model.
3. Receive the model's output probability.
4. Compare the result with the configured ML threshold.
5. Allow or block the setup according to the configuration.

8.2 Configuration

UseMLFilter = false by default.

MLModelPath = "BoltIQ_Gold_Filter.onnx"

MLThreshold can be configured within the supported range.

The ML filter can therefore be used as an optional trade-selection
mechanism rather than being required for the core strategy to operate.

8.3 ML Model Information

The existing product information describes the ML component as an
ONNX-based classification model using technical trading features.

However, no specific training-period, validation result, accuracy,
win-rate improvement, profit factor improvement or other historical
performance claim is made in this marketplace listing unless the
underlying training and validation evidence can be independently
reproduced.

This distinction is important: the presence of an ML model does not
guarantee better trading performance.

9. BUILT-IN RISK MANAGEMENT
===========================

BoltIQ Gold EA separates trading logic from account-level risk controls.

9.1 Daily Loss Protection

The EA monitors account equity against the configured daily-loss
threshold.

When the configured limit is reached, the risk engine can close
positions and block new trades.

9.2 Maximum Drawdown Protection

A second protection layer monitors account drawdown and can stop
further trading when the configured threshold is reached.

9.3 Configurable Position Sizing

Position size is calculated according to the configured risk percentage
and account equity.

Risk should be selected according to account size, drawdown limits and
applicable trading rules.

9.4 Session-Aware Risk Controls

The EA provides session-aware controls for major market periods,
including London and New York sessions.

9.5 Economic News Filter

The EA can pause trading around high-impact economic events using the
MetaTrader 5 built-in economic calendar.

The current product configuration references:

* NFP
* CPI
* FOMC
* ECB
* BoE

The configured protection window is 15 minutes before and after
high-impact events.

9.6 Position Limits

Position limits help prevent excessive simultaneous exposure and
overtrading.

============================================

An ML filter can change the number and characteristics of trades.

A different trade count, win rate or profit factor in one historical
test does not establish that the ML filter will improve live performance.

The ML model may also behave differently as market conditions change.

For this reason, traders should evaluate:

* The rule-based configuration.
* The ML-enabled configuration.
* The broker's actual execution conditions.
* The selected ML threshold.
* Out-of-sample or forward-test behavior.
* Risk and drawdown under the intended account rules.

No verified live or forward-test track record is claimed in this
marketplace listing.

5. HOW THE TRADING STRATEGY WORKS
============================================================

Gold Guardian does not enter a trade based on a single indicator.

The strategy combines trend direction, momentum and trend strength
before considering an entry.

5.1 Trend Direction

The EA uses the relationship between EMA(20) and EMA(50) to identify
potential bullish or bearish trend direction.

5.2 Momentum Confirmation

RSI(14) is used as a momentum filter.

Long setup: RSI(14) > 55

Short setup: RSI(14) < 45

5.3 Trend Strength Confirmation

ADX(14) must be above 25 before an entry is considered.

5.4 Volatility Management

ATR(14) is used to adapt stop-loss distance to current market
volatility.

============================================================ 
6. ENTRY CONDITIONS 
============================================================

Long Entry

-   EMA(20) crosses above EMA(50)
-   RSI(14) > 55
-   ADX(14) > 25

Short Entry

-   EMA(20) crosses below EMA(50)
-   RSI(14) < 45
-   ADX(14) > 25

Signals are based on closed bars.

8. BUILT-IN RISK MANAGEMENT
============================================================

Gold Guardian is designed with two distinct layers:

1.  Trading logic
2.  Risk-management controls

8.1 Daily Loss Protection

The EA monitors account equity against the configured daily-loss
threshold.

When the configured limit is reached, the risk engine can close
positions and block new trades.

8.2 Maximum Drawdown Protection

A second protection layer monitors maximum drawdown to help prevent
continued trading after the configured drawdown limit is reached.

8.3 Configurable Position Sizing

Position size is calculated using the configured risk percentage and
account equity.

The recommended risk level can be adjusted according to the trader’s
account and risk requirements.

8.4 Session-Aware Risk Controls

The EA provides session-aware risk management for major market periods,
including London and New York sessions.

Current configuration: - London Open: 08:00–09:00 UTC - New York Open:
13:00–14:00 UTC - London/New York overlap: 13:00–16:00 UTC

8.5 Economic News Filter

The EA can pause trading around high-impact economic events using the
MetaTrader 5 built-in economic calendar.

The current configuration references: - NFP - CPI - FOMC - ECB - BoE

The configured news protection window is 15 minutes before and after
high-impact events.

8.6 Position Limits

Position limits help prevent excessive simultaneous exposure and reduce
overtrading.

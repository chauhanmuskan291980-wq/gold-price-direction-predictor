2. SHORT MARKETPLACE INTRODUCTION
============================================================

Global Stop Loss and Target Setter is a free MetaTrader 5 utility for
manual traders who want to apply Stop Loss and Take Profit levels to
multiple open positions on the same symbol without editing each trade
individually.

With two on-chart buttons, traders can apply a fixed-distance Stop Loss
and Take Profit to all open positions on the current symbol, or move
profitable positions to break-even.

The utility is designed for manual position management. It only modifies
Stop Loss and Take Profit levels. It does not open, close, reverse or
place pending orders.

IMPORTANT: This is a position-management utility, not a trading strategy.
It does not generate trading signals or guarantee profits. Traders remain
responsible for verifying broker conditions and their account or prop-firm
rules.

5. HOW IT WORKS
============================================================

5.1 Apply SL and Target

Set the desired Stop Loss and Take Profit distances in points.

When the "Apply SL and Target" button is pressed, the utility applies
the configured levels to the open positions on the current symbol.

A Stop Loss or Take Profit distance set to zero can be used to leave
that protection level unchanged.

5.2 Break-Even

Press the "Break-Even" button to move eligible profitable positions'
Stop Loss to their entry price.

An optional break-even lock can move the Stop Loss beyond entry by the
configured number of points.

5.3 Current-Symbol Scope

The utility manages positions on the symbol of the chart where it is
running. It is intended to provide one-click management for the
positions associated with that symbol.

14. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

Q1. Is Global Stop Loss and Target Setter free?

Yes. It is provided as a free MetaTrader 5 utility.

Q2. Does it open trades?

No. It does not open trades or place pending orders.

Q3. Does it close trades?

No. Its purpose is to modify Stop Loss and Take Profit levels on
existing positions.

Q4. Can it manage several positions at once?

Yes. It can apply the configured SL and/or TP to open positions on the
current chart symbol.

Q5. Does it work with BUY and SELL positions?

Yes. BUY and SELL positions are handled according to their direction
when calculating the corresponding protection levels.

Q6. What does the Break-Even button do?

It moves the Stop Loss of eligible profitable positions to their entry
price, with an optional lock beyond entry.

Q7. Can I leave Stop Loss or Take Profit unchanged?

Yes. A distance of zero can be configured to leave that protection level
untouched.

Q8. Does it support hedging accounts?

Yes. Positions are addressed individually by ticket.

Q9. What happens if the requested SL/TP is too close to the market?

The broker's minimum stop-distance rules are respected. An invalid
modification may be skipped rather than affecting the other positions.

Q10. Does it work on Gold?

Yes. It can be used with Gold/XAUUSD, subject to the broker's symbol and
contract specifications.

Q11. Does it provide trading signals?

No. It is a position-management utility and does not generate trading
signals.

Q12. Does it automatically protect a new trade after I open it?

No. This free edition applies its settings when you use the controls.
For continuously active synchronization, use Global Stop Loss and
Target Manager.

Q13. Is this a prop-firm compliance tool?

It can help with manual position protection, but it does not guarantee
compliance with any prop-firm rules. Traders must verify and follow
their provider's current rules.

2. SHORT MARKETPLACE INTRODUCTION
=================================

Wyckoff Schematic Detector is a MetaTrader 5 indicator designed to
algorithmically identify selected Wyckoff-style market structures using
price range, volume and market-structure information.

The indicator analyzes trading ranges and attempts to identify four
broad market phases:

* Accumulation
* Markup
* Distribution
* Markdown

It can also identify configured Wyckoff-style events such as Springs,
Upthrusts, Last Point of Support (LPS), Back-Up (BU), Sign of Strength
(SOS) and Sign of Weakness (SOW).

The indicator combines price structure with volume analysis rather than
relying only on visual candlestick or price-pattern recognition.

IMPORTANT: Wyckoff Schematic Detector provides algorithmic analysis and
visualization. It does not guarantee profitable trades and does not
replace discretionary market analysis, risk management or trader
judgment.

Detection results can depend on symbol, timeframe, available volume
data, market conditions and indicator settings.

6. WYCKOFF PHASE DETECTION
==========================

6.1 ACCUMULATION

The current detection logic describes accumulation using combinations
of:

* Contracting price range.
* Relative volume behavior during downward moves.
* Spring detection.

The indicator displays the detected accumulation phase on the chart.

6.2 MARKUP

Markup detection uses combinations of:

* Higher-high / higher-low structure.
* Expanding volume during advances.
* Sign of Strength confirmation.

The indicator displays the detected markup phase and associated trend
information.

6.3 DISTRIBUTION

Distribution detection uses combinations of:

* Contracting price range.
* Relative volume behavior during upward moves.
* Upthrust detection.

The indicator displays the detected distribution phase.

6.4 MARKDOWN

Markdown detection uses combinations of:

* Lower-high / lower-low structure.
* Expanding volume during declines.
* Sign of Weakness confirmation.

The indicator displays the detected markdown phase.

IMPORTANT: These classifications are algorithmic interpretations based
on the configured rules. They should not be treated as definitive
statements about market intent.

7. KEY WYCKOFF EVENTS
=====================

SPRING

A potential Spring is identified when configured conditions indicate:

* Price moves below a detected range-support boundary.
* Volume behavior meets the configured condition.
* Price returns inside the trading range.
* The closing price confirms the return within the configured window.

A Spring marker is then displayed when the configured conditions are
satisfied.

UPTHRUST

A potential Upthrust is identified using the corresponding resistance
breakout and return-to-range conditions.

Typical configured conditions include:

* Price moves above range resistance.
* Volume meets the configured condition.
* Price returns inside the range.
* The close confirms the return within the configured window.

LPS — LAST POINT OF SUPPORT

The indicator can identify configured low-volume pullback conditions
associated with a potential Last Point of Support during markup.

BU — BACK-UP

The indicator can identify configured low-volume rally conditions
associated with a potential Back-Up during markdown.

SOS — SIGN OF STRENGTH

The indicator can identify configured volume-supported moves through
resistance associated with a potential Sign of Strength.

SOW — SIGN OF WEAKNESS

The indicator can identify configured volume-supported moves through
support associated with a potential Sign of Weakness.

IMPORTANT: These event labels represent algorithmic detection based on
configured criteria. They are not guaranteed trading signals.

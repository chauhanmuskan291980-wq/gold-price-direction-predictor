2. SHORT MARKETPLACE INTRODUCTION
=================================

Session Countdown Panel is a MetaTrader 5 indicator designed to make major trading sessions and configured market windows easier to monitor directly from the chart.

The indicator displays session information for Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York, together with countdown timers for session openings and closings.

It can also highlight configured kill zones such as the London open, New York open and London/New York overlap.

Session definitions, kill-zone windows, panel placement, colors, labels and alert settings can be customized according to the trader's preferred setup and broker time configuration.

The indicator is designed for traders who use session-based or time-sensitive trading strategies and want the relevant market windows visible without manually calculating session times.

IMPORTANT: Session Countdown Panel is a visual timing and alert tool. It does not generate trading signals, execute trades or guarantee profits. Session times can be affected by broker server time and daylight-saving changes. Traders should verify their broker's time configuration.

17. WHY SESSION TIMING SHOULD BE USED CAREFULLY
===============================================

Trading sessions are time-based market windows, but market behavior is not guaranteed to repeat in the same way every day.

Factors that can change market conditions include:

* Economic news.
* Market holidays.
* Daylight-saving changes.
* Broker trading hours.
* Liquidity conditions.
* Symbol-specific schedules.
* Unexpected market events.

The indicator therefore provides timing information rather than predicting what price will do during a session.

Traders should verify session definitions against their broker and trading strategy.

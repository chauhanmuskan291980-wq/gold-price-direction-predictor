5. HOW THE MULTI-TIMEFRAME STRATEGY WORKS
=========================================

The EA does not rely on a single timeframe to determine whether a trade should be taken.

Instead, the strategy uses three stages:

H1 → Market direction

M15 → Trend confirmation

M5 → Entry timing

This creates a structured top-down process where lower-timeframe entries are considered only after the higher-timeframe trend conditions are satisfied.

============================================================
6. TREND DEFINITION — H1
========================

The H1 timeframe represents the broader market direction.

Bullish H1 structure:

* EMA(20) > EMA(50) > EMA(200)
* EMA structure must be aligned upward according to the current strategy configuration.

Bearish H1 structure:

* EMA(20) < EMA(50) < EMA(200)
* EMA structure must be aligned downward according to the current strategy configuration.

Neutral H1 structure:

* Any other EMA arrangement.
* No directional setup is considered while the required alignment is absent.

The purpose of the H1 layer is to prevent lower-timeframe signals from being evaluated independently of the broader trend.

============================================================
7. M15 CONFIRMATION
===================

Once the H1 direction is established, the M15 timeframe is used as a confirmation layer.

The M15 EMA structure must agree with the H1 direction.

For a bullish setup:

* M15 EMA structure must support the bullish H1 direction.
* EMA(20) crossing above EMA(50) in the trend direction can arm the setup.

For a bearish setup:

* M15 EMA structure must support the bearish H1 direction.
* EMA(20) crossing below EMA(50) in the trend direction can arm the setup.

The M15 layer is therefore intended to reduce the number of M5 setups considered when the broader trend is unclear.

============================================================
8. M5 ENTRY TIMING
==================

The M5 timeframe is used for more precise entry timing.

For a bullish setup:

* The higher-timeframe trend must be bullish.
* The M15 confirmation must agree with the H1 direction.
* Price may pull back toward the M5 EMA(20) or EMA(50) area.
* A confirmation candle must close in the trend direction.
* Entry is considered on the next candle according to the strategy configuration.

For a bearish setup:

* The higher-timeframe trend must be bearish.
* The M15 confirmation must agree with the H1 direction.
* Price may pull back toward the M5 EMA(20) or EMA(50) area.
* A confirmation candle must close in the trend direction.
* Entry is considered on the next candle according to the strategy configuration.

The purpose of the M5 layer is to provide entry timing after the broader trend and confirmation conditions have been established.
